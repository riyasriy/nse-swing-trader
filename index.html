How the score is built
Each stock is scored 0–100 on five factors, then combined as a weighted average:

Trend (30%) — price above the 50-day average, 50-day above the 200-day average, and the 50-day average rising.
Momentum (25%) — RSI(14) peaking in the 55–65 zone (healthy, not yet overbought), plus a bonus when MACD is bullish.
Volume (20%) — today's volume relative to the 20-day average; confirms real participation, not a drift.
Volatility (15%) — ATR as a percent of price, favoring a 2–5% band: enough movement to swing-trade, not so much it's reckless.
Relative strength (10%) — 20-day return, ranked against every other stock scanned that day.

Stocks are pre-filtered to exclude prices under ₹20 and average daily turnover under ₹5 crore, to keep the list liquid and tradable. This is a ranking tool, not investment advice — always confirm with your own analysis before trading.
